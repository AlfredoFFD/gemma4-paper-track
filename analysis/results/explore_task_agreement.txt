EXPLORATORY: HOW FAR THE TASK DECIDES WHETHER A WHOLE RUN IS RESOLVED
EXPLORATORY, written after all results were seen; nothing here was registered. Four arms, one run per task
  and arm; the arms of 14 ran seed 1, those of 23 seed 2, in different launches.

1. Tasks: 103. Resolved per arm: 14 N 36, 14 NAF 37, 23 PE 38, 23 PH 35
2. Tasks by the number of arms (of four) in which they are resolved: 0 arms: 55, 1 arms: 9, 2 arms: 5, 3 arms: 9, 4 arms: 25
3. Tasks with the same outcome in all four arms: 80 of 103 (55 never resolved, 25 always); tasks on which the arms differ: 23
4. Share of the variance in resolved that lies between tasks: 0.804; intraclass correlation (one-way, four runs a task): 0.740
   the same correlation with the 412 outcomes shuffled over runs (2,000 shuffles, random.Random(0)): median -0.002, 97.5th percentile 0.090

In plain words: the four arms give the same outcome on 80 of 103 tasks; a comparison of the arms in resolved tasks rests on the other 23.
