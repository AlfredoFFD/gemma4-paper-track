JUDGMENT CALLS (where the registration left room)
  - Rows are mapped to steps and arms by (task, run, fork) against the states file; the fork id ends in the arm. record_arm and state_id are only checked against it. A repeated (step, arm, seed) keeps the first row.
  - API failure: api.error_kind "api". Usable call: rederive_19.failure_17 (the classes of rederive_15: runner error, no call, unknown tool, malformed; with the nine tools the agent declares known, the graph tools judged by fork_states.valid_call). A row with an error (an adapter JSON error) is a reply without a usable call.
  - Gate: usable rows divided by all rows of the model (API failures in the denominator, as "of its rows" reads); passed at 80% or more.
  - Scored files: re-derived from the recorded search of the ASKED record (NONE, TOLD, ASKED) and from the TOLDX note, repository .py files not opened earlier, as in rederive_20; compared with the stored hit_files.
  - USES THE LINE: rederive_15.score reads_hit_file and past_line_1. SEARCHES: a run_command whose first program is grep, egrep, fgrep, rg, ag, ack or git grep, as in rederive_20; find and search_similar_code are not searches there, and a variant counting them is printed beside (it matters on models that call search_similar_code).
  - Strict outcome (cut 3): USES THE LINE and the window read covers a hit line of the note (the scored lines of that file) give or take 10 lines. Window: read_file from start_line (1 if absent) to end_line, at most 150 lines (the window of rederive_15); a shell view by its known range. Taken as a subset of USES THE LINE ("strict twin"); first reads from line 1 whose window covers a hit line are counted and printed apart.
  - Cut 2: a step is a NONE-search step if at least two of its three NONE replays are usable and a search; it is computed on the steps complete for W2. A variant counts find and search_similar_code as searches.
  - Shares pool the replays of complete steps (three per arm). Each interval draws its own 2,000 resamples from a fresh random.Random(0). By task: the tasks of the complete steps are drawn with replacement, each with all its complete steps. The ratio leaves out a resample with ASKED at zero.
  - Verdicts: no verdict if the gate fails or fewer than 40 complete steps (then not met); W1 met if the lower bound is above 0; W2 "used less than asked" if the lower bound is above 0, "used more than asked" if the upper bound is below 0, else "no detectable difference"; W3 met if the upper bound is below 0.
  - Reference row: the rows of 20 scored by the same code, printed in the table, not counted. Its rows carry no api field, so no API failure can be detected there.
