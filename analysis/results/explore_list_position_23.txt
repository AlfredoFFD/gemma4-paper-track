JUDGMENT CALLS
 1. Lists, entries, 'a file not opened before', the scorer, the redefined next call (first tool call of the
    first model step after the step whose result carried the list) and 'within 3 calls' (call indexes p+1 to
    p+3) are those of analysis/explore_next_call_23.py, imported.
 2. Returned lines: end_line - start_line + 1 of the READ's result, the range the result reports (a read cut by
    the character limit can report more lines than its content holds). Characters: the length of the result's
    content field; the key follows the content in the result object.
 3. Source nodes: the length of the key's value in the trace for PE and PH (sources whose neighbors were all
    dropped are left out of the value, as the agent saw it); for N14 the length of the would-be value, recorded
    by wrapping push_23.Pusher.on_read for the duration of the replay.
 4. The thirds are cut on the pooled content characters of the three arms (percentiles with linear
    interpolation, rederive_18.percentile), so the same cut points apply to each arm. The fixed cuts by lines
    are those asked for; with the cap at 150, '150 or more' is 'exactly 150'.
