Ambiguities in the pre-registration texts and the readings taken here:
  A1. Interface failures: one class per row, checked in this order: error (non-empty error field), no call (next_call missing or empty), unknown tool (name outside the nine declared tools), malformed (fork_states.valid_call is False). Counts are over all rows of the run; rates use usable replays of complete states only.
  A2. Complete state: the state is in the states file and arms A, Aw, Hw and Ew each have a usable replay for seeds 1, 2 and 3. A repeated (state, arm, seed) usable row would be counted and the first one kept.
  A3. "Plain identifier" is the pattern [A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]* on the whole argument name.
  A4. "Quotes, backslashes and spaces are removed": the characters double quote, single quote, backslash and the space character are deleted anywhere in the name (not only at the ends); the result must then EQUAL start_line or end_line (not merely contain it). Backticks, tabs and newlines are not removed. A sensitivity count under two looser readings (also removing backticks; any non-identifier name containing start_line or end_line) is printed in the data checks.
  A5. F2 numerator: a usable replay whose first call is read_file and whose arguments hold at least one mangled line-bound name, whatever its value. A replay with two mangled names counts once.
  A6. F2 "reads with a line bound": usable first-call read_file replays whose arguments hold start_line or end_line with a non-null value, or a mangled line-bound name with a non-null value. The share among them is clustered by state over the states that have at least one such read in the arm.
  A7. F2 original runs: one observation per complete state (observed_next_call in the states file), scored with the same mangled-name rule, as a share of complete states and as a share of complete states whose observed call is a read with a line bound. The interval resamples states (each state is one cluster of size one). The observed call is used whatever its validity (all are valid in this states file).
  A8. Rates are percent of usable replays on complete states (each replay weight 1); the bootstrap resamples states with replacement and takes the ratio of summed hits to summed replays. Contrasts are differences of these ratios on the same resampled states (paired by state).
  A9. Bootstrap: numpy default_rng(0), 2,000 resamples; percentile bounds are order statistics b[int(0.025*n)] and b[int(0.975*n)-1] of the sorted resampled values. "Lower 95% bound above 0" is strict.
  A10. "Any unopened true neighbor touched": a replay counts if fork_states.touches returns read or edit for at least one name in the state's unshown_true_neighbors that has a span in neighbor_spans, with the file's line lengths from nodes.file_text (exact character caps); graph returns do not count. A read under a mangled bound name is scored as the registered scorer reads it (the misnamed bound is ignored).
  A11. F1: relative difference = (replayed - logged) / logged prompt tokens, computed here from the two token fields (the file's own diff field is not used); rows with an error or a missing or zero logged count are left out. "At most 0.5%" is inclusive. The mean and maximum are of the absolute relative differences.
  A12. Descriptive tool mix: the first call's tool name over usable replays on complete states (interface failures are shown separately in S0). Same tool as the original: next_call function_name equals observed_next_call function_name, over usable replays on complete states. Same file: among replays where both calls are read_file, the filepath strings are equal after stripping whitespace and a leading "./", "/workspace/" or "workspace/".
  A13. A partial run (fewer than 200 complete states) does not stop the script: the numbers are printed with an EXPLORATORY label.
