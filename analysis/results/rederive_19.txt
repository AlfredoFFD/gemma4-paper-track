JUDGMENT CALLS (where the registration left room)
  - API failure: a row whose api.error_kind is "api" (the runner sets it when the call got no reply after its retries). Every other row is a model reply. A row with a non-empty error and api.error_kind "adapter" got a reply whose tool call the harness could not parse: it is a reply without a valid tool call.
  - Valid tool call: design 10 by the classes of rederive_cd.py (error, no call, unknown tool among the nine declared tools, fork_states.valid_call false); design 17 by the classes of rederive_15.py (error, no call, unknown tool, arguments not an object or a required argument missing after cleaning the names; a mangled start_line is usable). The error class here holds only adapter errors, since API failures are taken out first. rederive_15.py lists six tools; the agent of 19 declares nine in both designs (its runner.log), so in design 17 a call to get_status, get_code_neighbors or get_code_subgraph is known and judged by fork_states.valid_call.
  - Gate: valid tool calls divided by replays that got a reply (API failures left out of the denominator); the share over all replays is printed beside it. Passed if at least 80% (inclusive). One gate per model and design.
  - Complete step: every arm and seed that was run has a usable replay: design 10 H and E, seeds 1 and 2 (4 replays); design 17 S, S0 and S0N, seeds 1 to 3 (9 replays). V2 is computed on these steps. Steps complete in S and S0 alone (the completeness of Q1 in pre-registration 15) are printed beside it as a variant, without a verdict.
  - Design 10 outcome "the next call reads a neighbor's code": the outcome of pre-registration 10 (M2), any unopened true neighbor touched, fork_states.touches returning read or edit for a name in unshown_true_neighbors, as rederive_cd.score_row computes it. The variant counting only "read" is printed beside.
  - Design 17 outcome Y1: a read of a hit file (read_file on it, or a shell view starting from cat/head/tail/sed -n with a known range) that starts past line 1, as rederive_15.score computes it; a start_line under a mangled argument name counts as asking.
  - Rates pool the replays of the steps (each replay weight 1). Bootstrap: numpy default_rng(0), 2,000 resamples of complete steps with replacement; per resample the difference of pooled rates on the same resampled steps; percentile bounds are the order statistics b[int(0.025 n)] and b[int(0.975 n) - 1] of the sorted values. Each contrast draws its own resamples from a fresh default_rng(0).
  - "Interval lies above zero": the lower 95% bound strictly above 0.
  - Verdicts in this order: "no verdict: gate" if the gate fails; "no verdict: cut" for design 17 if its run was stopped by a guard or has fewer rows than planned; "no verdict: floor" if fewer complete steps than the floor (200, 25); otherwise "met" or "not met".
  - Rows are mapped to steps by (task, run, fork): design 10 to the states of fork_states_d, design 17 to the state record whose fork id ends in :S, :S0 or :S0N (the record_arm field is checked against it). A repeated (step, arm, seed) keeps the first row and is counted.
  - Past line 1 among reads of a hit file (no rule) is printed per arm on complete steps and on all usable replays.
