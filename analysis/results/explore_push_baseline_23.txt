EXPLORATORY: A BASELINE FOR THE UPTAKE AND A1 NUMBERS OF PRE-REGISTRATION 23
EXPLORATORY. Written after the results of 23 were seen; nothing here was registered. The control is the stored N
  run of pre-registration 14: another launch and another seed (seed 1; PE and PH ran seed 2). Its pushed lists
  are replayed offline (analysis/modal_h/push_23.py on the reads that run made) and were seen by no agent. A
  real push changes what a run does next, so the would-be lists of the control are not the lists of PE or PH:
  the control's reads, and so its would-be lists, come from a run that never had a list to follow.

NOTES
 1. Entered runs and READ, START, A1 READ and uptake are rederive_23's (its section 9). For the control the
    would-be lists are put in place of its (empty) pushed lists, and A1 and uptake are computed on them with the
    same rules.
 2. The offline replay calls the Pusher on every read_file answer whose returned range is readable
    (push_23.shown_range), as explore_push_23.py and the runner do; rederive_23's READs are every read_file of
    status ok. Part 0's control A1 numbers are explore_push_23's count (its own START and file-opening rule);
    Parts 1 and 2 use rederive_23's.
 3. Per-run shares are taken over a run's entries (or A1 READs); a run with none has no share and its task drops
    out of that comparison. The pooled counts are on the same tasks as the paired means.
