5. JUDGMENT CALLS
  - Arm of a row: the suffix of the fork id of the record it names by (task, run, fork); NONE and ASKED both run as the runner arm A.
  - Scored files of NONE, TOLD and ASKED: re-derived from the last step of the ASKED record (the recorded search and its result) with the parser and the "opened" rule of rederive_15: repository .py files with a hit line, not opened earlier. TOLDX: parsed from its own note (path:line:text), repository files not opened earlier. Compared with the stored hit_files; the re-derived ones are used for scoring.
  - USES THE LINE: rederive_15.score on the scored files, reads_hit_file and past_line_1 (a read_file, or a shell view by cat/head/tail/sed -n, of a scored file, starting past line 1). A scored file is unopened by construction, so every such read is a first read. A start_line under a mangled name (equal to start_line once quotes, backslashes and blanks are removed) counts as written.
  - SEARCHES: a run_command whose first program is grep, egrep, fgrep, rg, ag, ack or git grep (find and search_similar_code not counted; printed apart under 4d).
  - Usable: not an interface failure under rederive_15 (runner error, no call, unknown tool, malformed call). A step is complete for a rule if both arms compared have usable calls for seeds 1 to 3.
  - Shares pool the replays of the steps (equal to the mean of per-step shares, since every complete step has three replays per arm). Each rule draws its own 2,000 resamples of steps from a fresh random.Random(0); percentile interval by linear interpolation at 2.5% and 97.5%.
  - T2 is decided on the point ratio (at least two thirds); its interval is printed. A resample with ASKED at zero is left out and counted. The ratio of 15's form leaves out a resample whose denominator is not above 0.
  - Per-source T3 uses the steps complete for T3, split by the stored toldx_source.
  - Next call when the line is not used: categories in this order: a read (as rederive_15 detects one) of any repository file with a hit line in the note, from line 1; the same past line 1 (possible only for a noted file opened before); a search; edit_file; anything else (write_file among it), with its kind.
  - Repeat of the recorded search: the next search command equals the recorded search command once every single and double quote is removed and runs of blanks are collapsed to one space (ends stripped).
  - ASKED against 15: rows of 15 whose fork is the state_of_15 of the step (<step>:S), scored with the same files and the same rules; compared on steps with three usable replays in both.
