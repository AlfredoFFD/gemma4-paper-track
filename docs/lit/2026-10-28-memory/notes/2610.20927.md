## What the read parts establish

- **The claim that matters for us** (abstract, §3.2, §7): compaction summaries mix facts the environment established with decisions and TODOs the compressor wrote, and "the agent cannot tell the two apart afterwards; it treats both as facts."
- **Evidence for it is observational** (§3.2, App. D): in 35 failed runs of an OpenClaw-style compressor on AppWorld training tasks, the failing action carries out a specific summary entry in 29; of those entries 22 are supported by no environment output, 3 contradict one, 4 are supported. Per scenario and pattern: 15 of 19. LLM annotators under a written protocol, two rounds (κ = 0.86 on support, 0.80 on pattern), authors resolved 14 disagreements.
- **Failure patterns** (§3.2): rewritten constraints (15 runs), invented deliverables (5), contradicted state (3), carried-forward choices or partial results presented as complete (6).
- **Intervention, end to end:** pruning unsupported entries with a judge model raises Pass^3 by 2.1–8.9 points over the same compressor on AppWorld and OfficeBench with two agent models (abstract, §7; the results section itself was not read).
- **Case study** (§5.5, Fig. 4 caption): a compressor-invented "omit meditation" entry → the agent deletes the line, failing 2 of 3 runs; pruned → 3 of 3 succeed. The same entry was present in the one successful unpruned run: "the unsupported entry raises the chance of the error rather than determining it."
- **Provenance is not truth** (Limitations, App. G): 92.2% of supported and 90.6% of unsupported entries are correct; only 24 of 298 labelled entries are incorrect; the source score ranks correct above incorrect entries no better than chance (AUROC 0.444). In failed runs, 4 of the 29 followed entries were supported but wrong.

## What it does not do (keyword check of the full text)

- No paired continuations from a saved state, no replays, no recompression arms: "counterfactual" appears only in the reference to PAIR; "replay", "restore", "paired" and "inject/insert/plant/fabricate" do not describe any experiment.
- No controlled insertion of a false entry, and no true-vs-false comparison at the same state. "Cannot tell the two apart" is inferred from attribution, not measured as discrimination.
- No code repository, code graph, small open model or next-action outcome.

## Overlap with the claims

- **Claim A:** not directly (it filters summary entries; it does not build a node-keyed memory).
- **Claim B:** partly. The closest published support for "agents act on wrong or unsupported memory entries after compaction", stated as a mechanism and backed by attribution plus an end-to-end pruning gain. It leaves open the controlled version: plant one wrong entry, hold everything else fixed, and compare following against a true entry at the same state.
