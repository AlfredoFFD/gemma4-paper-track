# Appendices to "A name is not an address, and an address is not enough"

Supplementary material (not part of the 3,000-word main text).

## Appendix

Supplementary material. Every number here is printed by a script in the repository from the raw outputs, and `analysis/reproduce.py` re-runs those scripts, except three sources: the counts of the four sets outside the competition (Appendix H; the script reads them through a dataset viewer on the network), the share of graph nodes that resolve (95.6%: 316,564 of 330,998 nodes over the 106 tasks, summed from the per-task counts of the tracked `analysis/fork_states_g/spans_manifest.json`, which the script that builds the spans writes) and the run times of table B (each run's `summary.json`). The main text names conditions in words; the scripts and this appendix use letters. A no list; P and H bare names as the tool's result and as a message; D bare names with an instruction to read them; E addresses (name, file, line range and first definition line); EL file and lines; ES name and definition line; EPATH file only; EG the address written as a search hit; PE the full entries inside the tool result; a prime marks the false-entry version; R_D and R_E carry a rewired list; a trailing w marks the earlier rendering of earlier tool results; AF and EF are A and E with misspelled line-bound names repaired; N, NA and NAF are as shipped, addressed, and addressed and repaired. After the agent's own search (pre-registrations 15, 17 and design 17 of 19), S, S0 and S0N are its search result as recorded, with its line numbers removed, and with them removed and handed back in a note. For the task prompt (pre-registration 16), C is the current task prompt, CS the same with the September wording of the four lines on the graph tools, S the September task prompt and SC the same with the current wording. S thus names an arm of both; each use below says which pre-registration it belongs to. In the runs told where the fix is (pre-registrations 18 and 22), N is the control (the harness as shipped, nothing added), ON the places of the fix told as names (the "names" arm of the main text), OA the same places as files and line ranges (the "lines" arm) and OF the named functions with their own line ranges. In pre-registration 20 (asked or told), NONE adds nothing, TOLD hands over the hits the agent's search was about to return, TOLDX the hits of another search, and ASKED is the step as recorded, after the agent's own search. Design 10 and design 17 of pre-registration 19 are the replays of pre-registrations 10 (H against E) and 15 (S, S0, S0N) on hosted models. Some words here are older than the main text's: a state is a paused step; mangled means misspelled; touched means its code was read; the gold patch and gold nodes are the reference patch and the fix; the decoy is the false entry and the swapped-out entry the true entry it replaced; honored means repaired; a note is a message; v28 and the wheels of Sep 30 are the harness of Sep 30, the wheels of Oct 12 the current harness; the original rendering is the earlier rendering; hosted and rented are the two GPU platforms (the hosted API of pre-registration 19 is a third, without a GPU of ours); one step is one call; an interface failure is a call that is not well formed (a usable call is one that is); a full address is an address; an arm is a condition; and a session, in whole runs, is a run.

### A. Pre-registered rules and outcomes (every rule and gate; Table 2 of the main text is a digest)

The twenty-three files of the registered experiments (02 to 20 and 22 to 25; there is no 21, F.36) hold 78 rules, 15 gates (a gate is a check that a run is usable, labelled as such below) and three readings fixed across rules (the last rows of 23, of 24 and of 25). A rule with two registered comparisons, each with its own verdict, counts as two (M1 of 05, on two models; U3 of 18; L5, L6 and L7 of 22; H1 of 23); V1 and V2 of 19 and W1 to W3 of 24 count once each and were judged on seven models. Of the 78 rules: met 34 (among them C2 of 04, counted once, and K2 of 25, whose registered words are "the place matters"); not met 13 (among them B4 of 03, which is met as coded and not met with the registered filter, and A1 and A2 of 23 and K1 of 25, whose registered word is "not detected"); one harm rule not triggered (K2 of 06); no detectable difference 8 (among them A3 of 23); a direction found 2 (X2: asked for less often; W3 of 16: more graph calls with the September listing); the second of two registered labels 1 (T2: "telling does not replace asking"); not tested or underpowered 2; no verdict 14 (ten of pre-registration 22: one shard of two ran, 20 tasks under a floor of 34; four of 23: P1, P2 and the two comparisons of H1, since its launch ran no control); judged model by model 3 (W1 to W3 of 24: met on seven, two and five of the seven models; W3 on four by the second script). ### D. Interface failures and the mangled argument name

Malformed calls, calls to unknown tools and responses without a call are excluded from every estimate and counted in table B.

**Cause, rate and repair** (Section 3; the paragraph of the earlier main text). The 31B model sometimes writes a line bound under a misspelled argument name, `start_line"`, with a stray quote copied from tool results that the harness renders as dictionaries. The agent framework drops an argument that the tool does not declare, so the read silently returns lines 1 to 150. *Cause.* At paused steps such reads are 18.3% of next calls when earlier tool results are rendered as dictionaries, as the harness has done since Sep 30, and 0.2% under the earlier rendering (pre-registration 07, rule F2: +18.17 [14.75, 21.75], met). *Rate.* In fresh episodes as shipped, which end after eight calls, 13.2% of the reads that ask for a line range carry a misspelled bound (66 of 501; pre-registration 08, rule N1, met). Over whole runs as shipped the share is 3.8% (27 of 716; `analysis/results/14/analyze_14.txt`). In the whole runs of pre-registration 18 it is 8 of 301 ranged reads (2.7%) in the control, 16 of 361 (4.4%) with names and 9 of 388 (2.3%) with lines (`analysis/results/18/analyze_18.txt`). *Repair and its cost.* The repair renames an argument of `read_file` that reads `start_line` or `end_line` once quotes, backslashes and spaces are removed, before the tool runs (Appendix G, NAF). In five-call replays on the current harness it raises the share of replays shown the fix from 28.2% to 33.7% (pre-registration 08, rule G2: +5.49 [2.35, 8.82], met), an upper estimate: paused steps follow a file read, where the defect is most common. In whole runs with both changes a bound was restored in 17 runs (89 reads).

### G. Wording of the notes

- **H:** `Code graph note from the harness. get_code_neighbors(node="...") returned:` followed by the tool's JSON, `{"status": "ok", "node": "...", "neighbors": ["pkg.mod.name", ...]}`.
- **D:** `Instruction from the harness. Before you edit anything, look at the code graph neighbors of "..." below and read the ones you have not opened yet.` followed by the same JSON.
- **E:** H's sentence, with each neighbor as `{"name": "pkg.mod.name", "file": "pkg/mod.py", "lines": "272-278", "def": "def name(...) -> ...:"}`. EL drops `def`; ES drops `file` and `lines`; EPATH keeps `name` and `file` only.
- **P and PE:** the same JSON, bare or with full entries, as the result of an inserted `get_code_neighbors` call.
- **NA and NAF** (fresh episodes): `get_code_neighbors` returns `"neighbors": [{"name": "pkg.mod.name", "file": "pkg/mod.py", "lines": "272-278"}, ...]`; `get_code_subgraph` the same for `"nodes"`; each result of `search_similar_code` reads `{"node_name": ..., "file": ..., "lines": ..., "code": ..., "similarity": ...}`. An entity that does not resolve in the snapshot keeps its name only (95.6% of the graph nodes of the 106 tasks resolve). NAF also renames an argument of `read_file` that reads `start_line` or `end_line` once quotes, backslashes and spaces are removed, before the tool runs.
- **TOLD and TOLDX** (pre-registration 20): `Matches in the repository:` followed by hit lines as the agent's search printed them (file, line number, text), attached the way the note of S0N is attached.
- **ON, OA and OF** (pre-registrations 18 and 22): the task statement the agent receives ends with `Note: the accepted fix for this issue changes code in these places:`, then one line per place. ON: the qualified name of the smallest function or class that contains the place's first changed line (the module's dotted name when there is none, the file path when the file is not Python). OA: the file path, the place's line range in the task's snapshot (the changed region padded by 10 lines on each side, merged with neighbors within 5 lines), and the same name in parentheses. OF: one line per distinct name of the ON note, with the file path and the line range of that function or class (the file path and the name alone for a module).
